The Boy From Heppenheim

Sebastian Vettel's journey in Formula 1 is a story of relentless passion, historic records, and a profound love for the sport. Debuting in 2007, Vettel quickly established himself as a prodigy. He claimed his first pole position and race victory at the 2008 Italian Grand Prix in a Toro Rosso, becoming the youngest driver to do so at the time. This breathtaking performance in torrential rain signaled the arrival of a future legend.

Moving to Red Bull Racing in 2009, Vettel partnered with the genius of Adrian Newey to dominate the early 2010s. Between 2010 and 2013, he achieved a staggering four consecutive World Drivers' Championships. His 2013 season remains one of the most dominant in the sport's history, marked by nine consecutive race victories—a record that stood untouched for nearly a decade.

Later in his career, Vettel made the emotional move to Scuderia Ferrari, aiming to emulate his childhood hero, Michael Schumacher. While the championship in red proved elusive, Vettel became one of Ferrari's most successful and beloved drivers, securing 14 victories for the team. In his final years with Aston Martin, Vettel transitioned into a respected elder statesman of the grid, passionately advocating for environmental issues and human rights.

"Everybody is a Ferrari fan. Even if they say they're not, they are Ferrari fans." — Sebastian Vettel
